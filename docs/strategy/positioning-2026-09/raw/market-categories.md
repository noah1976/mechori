# Raw Market Categories: MECHORI
*Skill: startup-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-22*

## Candidate frames

| Category | Type | Buyer expectation | Fit | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance Record App | Existing | Log work, mileage, cost, reminders, receipts | Moderate | Frames MECHORI as personal storage; misses Workshop roundtrip |
| Digital Service History | Existing | Consolidated and often Workshop-originated service record | Moderate | “Official/verified/complete” expectations exceed current α |
| Vehicle Maintenance Passport / 愛車の整備履歴パスポート | Subcategory of digital service history | Portable history shown across Workshops, with Owner control | Strongest | “Passport” alone is ambiguous and category awareness is low |
| Workshop Collaboration Tool | Existing/adjacent | Jobs, customers, forms, team workflow, integrations | Weak for entry | Workshop buyer/value is unvalidated; table stakes are missing |
| Vehicle History Service | Existing | Accident/title/ownership/odometer/service lookup | Weak | Suggests authoritative third-party coverage and resale verification |
| Vehicle Health Record | New/adjacent | Health status, diagnosis, inspection, clinical-record analogy | Weak | Implies current condition or diagnostic authority |
| Hobby Car Maintenance Platform | Subcategory | Broad record, community, parts, help, Workshop support | Moderate | Too broad to claim one mental rung |
| Automotive Knowledge Network | New category | Searchable collective intelligence and network effects | Long-term only | Aspirational; present knowledge density and reuse are unproven |
| Mechanic Professional Network | Existing/adjacent | Identity, reputation, jobs, consultation, peer network | Long-term only | Mechanic adoption and reputation evidence are unproven |

## Market evidence

**[Data]** CARFAX frames Car Care as a free car-maintenance tracker with service history, reminders, recalls, shops, and vehicle value. This shows the “maintenance tracker/service history” category is legible but crowded and expectation-rich.

**[Data]** Digital Servicebook frames itself as a multi-brand digital service book where a Workshop registers service and history follows the car across Owners, Workshops, dealers, and buyers. This validates the portability job but also raises verification, integration, and warranty expectations MECHORI cannot currently claim.

**[Data]** FAINES frames itself as a database for automotive repair businesses containing manuals, technical information, service data, and repair cases. This confirms repair-information databases are a separate category from an Owner-carried individual vehicle history.

## Recommendation

Use **愛車の整備履歴パスポート** in Japanese and **Vehicle Maintenance Passport** in English as an entry subcategory. Always attach the plain-language explanation “工場へ持ち運べる整備履歴” until comprehension is demonstrated.

Do not claim category creation. The underlying buyer frame is digital service history / maintenance record; “Passport” is the qualifier that makes portability and roundtrip use salient.

## Data gaps

- No evidence that Japanese Owners spontaneously search for “愛車パスポート” or “整備履歴パスポート.”
- No full global category map; checked examples are the US, Europe, and Japan.
- No direct comparison against every local maintenance-record app.

## Red Flags

- “Digital Service History” can imply completeness and verification.
- “Vehicle History Service” invites a CARFAX-like comparison MECHORI cannot meet.

## Yellow Flags

- The English category may need market-specific copy while retaining the same job.
