# Market Category Analysis: MECHORI
*Skill: startup-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-22*

## Category decision criteria

A useful entry category must let a first-time Owner or Workshop predict the job without understanding MECHORI's architecture or long-term network. It should frame the current product honestly, make the differentiator visible, and avoid table-stakes MECHORI does not yet meet.

## Candidate categories

### 1. 整備記録アプリ / Maintenance Record App

- **Type:** Existing category, head-to-head.
- **Buyer expectations:** logging, mileage, cost, receipts, reminders, export.
- **Fit:** Moderate.
- **Advantage:** immediately understandable; matches current Garage capability.
- **Problem:** makes the Workshop roundtrip look like an extra feature and invites competition on reminders, fuel, cost, and polish.

### 2. Digital Service History

- **Type:** Existing category, head-to-head.
- **Buyer expectations:** consolidated, durable, often Workshop-originated or verified history; sometimes warranty and resale support.
- **Fit:** Moderate.
- **Advantage:** validates the idea that history should follow a car across parties.
- **Problem:** CARFAX and Digital Servicebook show that the category carries data-network, verification, completeness, and integration expectations MECHORI cannot currently meet.

### 3. 愛車の整備履歴パスポート / Vehicle Maintenance Passport

- **Type:** Subcategory of maintenance record / digital service history.
- **Buyer expectations:** an Owner-controlled object that travels to the Workshop and returns with the result.
- **Fit:** Strongest.
- **Advantage:** foregrounds portability and the roundtrip while remaining anchored in “整備履歴.”
- **Problem:** “Passport” is not self-explanatory alone and may be mistaken for a static profile or official document.

### 4. Workshop Collaboration Tool

- **Type:** Existing/adjacent B2B category.
- **Buyer expectations:** accounts, team roles, jobs, customers, integrations, work orders, operational reliability.
- **Fit:** Weak for entry.
- **Advantage:** makes the two-sided workflow explicit.
- **Problem:** Workshop adoption and buyer value are unvalidated; current α intentionally lacks Workshop accounts and full operations.

### 5. Vehicle History Service

- **Type:** Existing category.
- **Buyer expectations:** authoritative lookup of accident, title/ownership, odometer, service, and resale history.
- **Fit:** Weak.
- **Advantage:** broadly understandable in markets with established providers.
- **Problem:** implies third-party coverage and verification beyond an Owner-controlled maintenance record.

### 6. Vehicle Health Record

- **Type:** Emerging/new category.
- **Buyer expectations:** current condition, inspections, diagnostic signals, or a reliable health state.
- **Fit:** Weak.
- **Advantage:** expresses longitudinal care.
- **Problem:** creates medical/diagnostic and quality-guarantee implications that violate MECHORI's safety and evidence boundaries.

### 7. Hobby Car Maintenance Platform

- **Type:** Broad subcategory.
- **Buyer expectations:** logs, community, advice, parts, Workshops, perhaps marketplace.
- **Fit:** Moderate as a company descriptor, weak as entry positioning.
- **Advantage:** aligns with best-fit customer and global hobby-car vision.
- **Problem:** too broad to tell a first-time user what to do now.

### 8. Automotive Knowledge Network / 整備Knowledge Network

- **Type:** Category creation.
- **Buyer expectations:** content density, search, trusted contributors, network effects.
- **Fit:** Weak now; plausible long term.
- **Advantage:** aligns with the mission of preserving experience for the next Owner and mechanic.
- **Problem:** current Knowledge reuse, density, and cross-vehicle value are hypotheses.

### 9. Mechanic Professional Network / 整備士向けProfessional Network

- **Type:** Existing/adjacent professional-network category.
- **Buyer expectations:** verified identity, reputation, portfolios, jobs, peer collaboration.
- **Fit:** Weak now; long-term layer only.
- **Advantage:** frames the “整備士のGitHub” thesis.
- **Problem:** no validated mechanic adoption, contribution rights, reputation value, or revenue.

## Recommendation

### Entry category

**愛車の整備履歴パスポート**

Required plain-language descriptor:

> **工場へ持ち運べる整備履歴**

English working category:

> **Vehicle Maintenance Passport**

This is a subcategory strategy, not category creation. Use familiar “maintenance history” semantics and let “Passport” identify the narrower cross-Workshop, return-to-history job.

### Value anchor

Do not compare value to “a better social network” or “a smarter AI.” Compare it to the cost and uncertainty of reconstructing the vehicle story from LINE, memory, invoices, and photos at every handoff.

### Buyer-expectation alignment

Meet:

- vehicle-specific chronological record;
- shareable Workshop context;
- Owner control and revocation;
- returned work preserved with source distinction.

Explicitly manage:

- not complete;
- not officially verified by default;
- not a diagnosis or health guarantee;
- not a replacement for repair manuals, FAINES, DMS, work orders, or invoices.

## Why not use the long-term category now?

Knowledge Network and Professional Network explain where accumulated evidence might go, not what the first Owner can reliably receive today. Leading with them would force MECHORI to educate a market while defending capabilities and network density that do not yet exist.

## Data gaps

## Confidence

**Medium** for the recommended frame as the best available test; **Low** for proven unaided category comprehension.

## Red Flags

- “Passport” without “整備履歴” may be read as a profile, ownership document, or travel metaphor.
- “Digital Service History” can overstate completeness and verification.

## Yellow Flags

- The category may need localized descriptors while retaining the same core job.
- The best category could revert to the simpler “maintenance record app” if Workshops do not use the roundtrip.
