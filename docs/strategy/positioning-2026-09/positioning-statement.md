# Positioning Statements: MECHORI
*Skill: startup-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-22*

## Moore positioning statement

**For 過去整備が紙・明細・写真・メモに散在し、新しい／追加の工場へ履歴を伝える必要がある、長期所有の趣味車・旧車・希少車・輸入車Owner, who need to carry forward what has already happened to the car, MECHORI is an 愛車の整備履歴パスポート that lets them share relevant past work and bring the Workshop's returned result back into the same history. Unlike LINE／口頭で今回を伝え、紙・明細・写真で過去を持つ運用, MECHORI turns each Workshop handoff into Owner-reviewed history available for the next maintenance visit.**

## Neumeier Onliness statement

### Internal hypothesis; not approved public copy

> MECHORI is the only 愛車の整備履歴パスポート that carries Owner-approved past work into a Workshop and brings the returned result back for the next Workshop.

**Result: Needs evidence.** The workflow is distinct from the primary status quo, but LIGHT research cannot substantiate a global “only” claim, and the crucial history-enabled flow is in unmerged PR #26. Do not publish “唯一.”

## Extended onliness test

- **WHAT:** 愛車の整備履歴パスポート / Vehicle Maintenance Passport.
- **HOW:** Owner-approved past history goes to the Workshop; the returned work comes back into the same vehicle history.
- **WHO:** Owners of history-sensitive hobby, older, rare, imported, or modified cars whose records are scattered and who must brief a new or additional Workshop.
- **WHERE:** Validation in Japan; product and data design intended to generalize globally without treating Japanese inspection or Workshop practice as universal.
- **WHY:** To reduce repeated reconstruction of the vehicle's story and preserve each visit for the next.
- **WHEN:** When service information is increasingly digital but remains fragmented across Owner, Workshop, manufacturer, paper, and messaging channels.

## Mental-ladder claim

> **整備履歴を、次の工場へ。**

One rung: portable maintenance continuity. It does not claim the best SNS, the best diagnosis, or a complete Workshop system.

## Elevator pitch

MECHORIは、趣味車や旧車を長く乗る人のための、工場へ持ち運べる整備履歴パスポートです。紙や明細、写真に散らばった過去整備と今回の相談をひとつのリンクで渡し、工場から返った作業内容はオーナーが確認して愛車の履歴へ戻せます。次の入庫でも、説明をゼロからやり直さないための仕組みです。

## Two-sentence test

> MECHORIは、趣味車・旧車などを長く乗るオーナーのための、工場へ持ち運べる愛車の整備履歴パスポートです。過去の整備と今回の相談を共有し、工場から返った作業内容をオーナーが確認して次回へ残すことで、「入庫のたびに説明をゼロからやり直す」を減らします。

**Restatement target:** “車ごとの過去整備を工場に見せて、今回の作業も次回へ残すサービス.” If first-time viewers cannot restate both directions—history goes out and result comes back—the position is not yet understood.

## One-line variants

- **Website hero:** 工場へ見せて、今回の整備も次回へ残せる。愛車の整備履歴パスポート。
- **α invitation:** 次の入庫で使う、あなたの愛車の整備履歴を試してください。
- **Workshop-facing page:** オーナーが共有した、今回の相談とこれまでの整備履歴です。
- **Professional teaser:** 顧客車両の過去を受け取り、今回の作業結果をオーナーへ返す。
- **Social bio:** 愛車の整備履歴を、次の工場・次の整備へ。

## Validation notes

- **Onliness Test:** Needs evidence; public “only” claim fails.
- **Moore Template:** Pass as a testable statement; every field is specific.
- **Mental Ladder:** Pass; one memorable rung is portability to the next Workshop.
- **Overall positioning strength:** Moderate / experiment-ready.

## Data gaps

- Unaided comprehension, preference versus LINE, and later reuse are unmeasured.
- “Passport” category comprehension outside Japan is untested.

## Red Flags

- Do not remove the “整備履歴” descriptor and assume “愛車パスポート” explains itself.
- Do not use the Onliness statement as a factual marketing claim.

## Yellow Flags

- “説明をゼロからやり直さない” must be understood as reducing repeated explanation, not guaranteeing a Workshop has read or accepted the record.
